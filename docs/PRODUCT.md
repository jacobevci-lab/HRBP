# HRBP One — Product Direction

## Positioning
HRBP One is a complete enterprise HCM / HRMS system of record. It owns the employee lifecycle from candidate to alumni while exposing integrations to identity, finance, government, banking and collaboration systems.

## Product principles
1. One person / one employee golden record.
2. Positions and people are separate first-class objects.
3. Effective-dated history is preserved; important facts are never overwritten.
4. Workflow, audit, privacy, retention and authorization are platform capabilities, not per-module add-ons.
5. Sensitive employee-relations and special-category data live behind additional policy boundaries.
6. AI assists decisions; it does not autonomously terminate, promote, discipline or rate employees.
7. External integrations may consume or enrich records, but HRBP One remains the HR system of record.

## Functional domains
Core HR; Organization & Position Management; Recruiting; Preboarding & Onboarding; Time & Attendance; Leave; Payroll; Compensation; Benefits; Performance & Goals; Talent; Succession; Skills; Learning; Engagement; Employee Relations; HR Service; Documents & e-Sign; Policies & Acknowledgement; Workforce Planning; Org Design; People Analytics; Workflow Automation; Privacy & Compliance; Audit; AI Assistant.
